REDEMPTION ROAD - CHAPTER 1: The Memorial and the Memory of Flight
SS1 SUMMARY - Quick Summary
In Chapter 1, Bendu Lewis and her family hold a memorial service for her grandmother, Catherine May Tyler Lewis (Granny May), 10 years after she died during the Liberian civil war. Her body was never found, so they use a casket with her Bible, pictures, and Liberian flag inside. During the service, Bendu remembers how she tried to save Granny May in a rusty wheelbarrow while fleeing from rebels in Charlue Town.

Detailed Summary for WAEC, NECO, JAMB

Chapter 1 opens with the 10th anniversary memorial of Bendu's paternal grandmother, Catherine May Tyler Lewis. The Lewis family could not recover her remains because she died undignified — "emaciated legs and arms dangling over the side of a dirty, rusty wheelbarrow" during the war. They place a symbolic casket with her portrait, her Bible, a photo of her late husband Samuel Lewis II, a white rose, and the Liberian flag.

Family and friends gather, including Bendu's parents Benjamin and Eva Lewis who returned from the USA just for this memorial. Also present is Bendu's older sister Siatta and her husband Terrance Clarke.

Key WAEC Points:
- Bendu feels deep guilt and cannot tell her family the full truth about Granny May's death
- Theme: Trauma, memory, survivor's guilt
- Bendu as selfless — she refused to abandon Granny May
- Setting: Post-war Monrovia, Liberia under Charles Taylor era
