Redemption Road by Elma Shaw - Chapter 1
Chapter 1 - The Memorial
SS1 QUICK SUMMARY:

Bendu attends a memorial service for her grandmother Granny May who died almost 10 years ago during the Liberian civil war. Bendu remembers how she pushed Granny May in a rusty wheelbarrow to escape war, Granny May died on the road, and Bendu was captured by rebel fighters Commander Cobra and Samson who broke her finger. She has a secret about one year she spent with the fighters.
REDEMPTION ROAD BY ELMA SHAW - CHAPTER 1 SUMMARY What Happens in Chapter 1: The chapter begins at a memorial service for Catherine May Tyler Lewis, also called Granny May. She died almost ten years ago during the Liberian civil war, and her body was never found. Her family holds a service with an empty casket. Inside the casket, they place her portrait, her Bible, family pictures, a picture of her husband Samuel Lewis II, a white rose, and the Liberian flag. The main character is Bendu Lewis, Granny May's granddaughter. Bendu is in deep pain because she was the last person to see her grandmother alive. She remembers how Granny May died. The chapter then goes back in time to the war: Bendu's Family Background: Bendu's parents, Ben and Eva Lewis, left Liberia in the early 1990s when Charles Taylor escaped from an American prison and entered Liberia to fight President Samuel K. Doe. After nine years of war, Taylor became President. Ben and Eva lost all their property and returned from America only for this memorial. They are staying with their older daughter Siatta and her husband Terrance Clarke. The Escape: During the war, Bendu was pushing her sick grandmother, Granny May, in a dirty, rusty wheelbarrow as they escaped from Charlue Town. Everyone was running for their lives. People shouted at Bendu to leave the old woman and run. Along the road, Bendu saw abandoned babies crying, small children being trampled, and old and sick people left behind crying for help. Bendu refused to leave her grandmother. She pushed until her hands had blisters and her legs were sore. Finally, Granny May whispered, "Leave me baby. Go. Save yourself!" Bendu said she would never leave her. Then Granny May's body went limp and she died. An old man on the road told Bendu, "You did well to stay with your Ma. God will bless you." The Rebels Arrive: Immediately after Granny May died, about thirty rebel fighters appeared. They were not the official Liberian army. Some wore wigs and some were very young boys. Their leaders were Commander Cobra - a tall, muscular man with a moustache - and Samson, a man with dreadlocks. Bendu was tired of the war and tired of running. She wanted to die so she could join her late fiancé Jonah in heaven. She was not afraid and even sucked her teeth at them. Samson wanted to hit her, but Commander Cobra stopped him. When they asked her name, she said "Bendu Lewis." They called her a "Congo girl" and "Americo-Liberian." Commander Cobra was surprised that she could speak their local dialect. She said she learned it at the University of Liberia. The fighters laughed at her for carrying a dead body and ordered her to follow them. Bendu held the wheelbarrow and refused. Samson hit her finger hard with his gun until it broke with a crack. The men dragged her away screaming. She fainted from pain, hunger, and shock. When she woke up, it was dark and the fighters were setting up camp in the woods. Back to the Present: The chapter ends back at the memorial service. Bendu is surrounded by her family and friends from the peace education center, like Agnes, Tenneh, and Calvin Daniels. But she feels alone. She has a secret about what happened during the one year she spent with the fighters, and she is afraid her family will not understand or forgive her if they know. She feels she cannot forget the war, even though other survivors say "forget it" and "let bygones be bygones." Characters in Chapter 1: 1. Bendu Lewis - The main character, Granny May's granddaughter 2. Catherine May Tyler Lewis (Granny May) - Bendu's grandmother 3. Benjamin and Eva Lewis - Bendu's parents 4. Commander Cobra and Samson - Rebel commanders Theme: Love, loss, trauma of war, and survival.
